Project 01 · Biped

Sports Player Rig

Full body and facial control rig for a stylised sports game character

Rig showreel · 2026

What's in the rig

Controls and deformation
  • IK legs with knee pole targets and heel, ball & toe foot roll
  • FK arms with an upper-arm twist control and 3 joints on every finger
  • Hips, spine & chest controls, plus a 3-bone ponytail chain
  • Facial rig with blinking eyelids, brows and lip-corner controls
  • Opening jaw with modelled teeth, gums and a 2-bone tongue
  • Clean weights so the clothing moves properly with the body

Project 02 · Quadruped

Horse Rig

Quadruped control rig with a seamless gallop cycle

Horse rig showreel · 2026

What's in the rig

Controls, deformation and animation
  • IK on all four legs with hoof controls and knee & hock pole targets
  • 4-bone spine and 2-bone neck with head and ear controls
  • 4-bone tail animated with overlapping follow-through
  • Colour-coded controls: left in blue, right in red, centre in yellow
  • Game-ready weights: smoothed joints, normalised, max 4 bones per vertex
  • Seamless gallop: a 54-frame loop with grounded hooves

About.

Aamir Mazhar

CGI Artist & 3D Rigger

I'm Aamir Mazhar, a CGI artist from Multan, Pakistan. Since 2021 I've worked at Topedge Technologies, creating ad creatives for mobile games on Mintegral and Google Ads, and store listings for the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

I also build 3D character rigs in Blender, with clean controls for animators and deformation that holds up in motion. My background is in graphic design and social media, and I hold a BS in Information Technology.
